Privacy Policy

How Navisignal Insights collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: May 2025

Navisignal Insights ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Joelle Petrus, an independent advisory and consulting practice based in Washington, D.C. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit navisignal.app/insights or any related web pages (collectively, the "Site") and when you engage with our services, newsletter, or resources.

By using this Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Site.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill out a contact or inquiry form (name, email address, message)
  • Subscribe to our newsletter (first name, email address, interest topics)
  • Book a consultation call via our scheduling tool
  • Download a resource or template (email address)
  • Complete a payment for a paid resource or service (handled by a third-party processor)

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on those pages
  • Referring URL
  • IP address (anonymized where possible)
  • Device type and operating system

This information is collected through cookies and analytics tools. See Section 5 for more detail.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide advisory or consulting services
  • Send you our newsletter and occasional field notes (only if you have subscribed)
  • Deliver resources, templates, or downloads you have requested
  • Schedule and manage consultation calls
  • Process payments for paid resources or services
  • Improve the content, functionality, and performance of the Site
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for automated profiling or decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for newsletter subscriptions and marketing communications
  • Legitimate interests — for responding to contact inquiries and improving Site performance
  • Contractual necessity — for delivering services or resources you have requested or purchased
  • Legal obligation — where required by applicable law

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party platforms to operate the Site and deliver our services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs their data practices:

  • Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — email newsletter and subscriber management. Kit stores your name and email address when you subscribe. Kit Privacy Policy
  • HubSpot — scheduling tool for booking consultation calls. HubSpot may collect name, email, and meeting details. HubSpot Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — payment processing for paid resources or services. Navisignal Insights does not store your payment card details; these are handled entirely by Stripe. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — web fonts loaded from Google servers, which may collect your IP address as part of serving font files. Google Privacy Policy

We take reasonable steps to ensure these third parties handle your data in a manner consistent with applicable privacy laws.

5. Cookies

This Site may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve functionality and understand how visitors use the Site. Types of cookies we may use include:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the Site to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies — to understand traffic patterns and improve the Site (e.g., page views, session duration)
  • Third-party cookies — set by embedded tools such as HubSpot scheduling widgets

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain Site functionality. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Specifically:

  • Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe
  • Contact inquiry data is retained for up to 2 years
  • Payment records are retained as required by applicable tax and financial regulations

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time (via the unsubscribe link in any email)
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@navisignal.app. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and no later than 30 days.

8. International Data Transfers

Navisignal Insights is based in the United States. If you are accessing the Site from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for international data transfers.

9. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately at hello@navisignal.app.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Links to Other Websites

The Site may contain links to third-party websites, including navisignal.app and external standards bodies, frameworks, or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@navisignal.app
  • Website: navisignal.app/insights — Contact Page
