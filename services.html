Services & advisory offers

Practical advisory support for sustainability, supply chain, and responsible AI decisions.

Navisignal Insights helps organizations move from vague goals and scattered information to clear priorities, practical systems, and decision-ready outputs.

Corporate Sustainability & ESG Readiness

For companies navigating sustainability expectations and accountability.

Sustainability Strategy & Decision Readiness

Advisory support to clarify sustainability priorities, define decision criteria, and translate broad commitments into practical next steps.

What you get

A structured strategy memo, priority map, implementation roadmap, and recommended decision points.

Best for

Companies that know sustainability matters but need clearer priorities, sequencing, and internal alignment.

Impact Measurement & KPI Design

Design of practical KPI, indicator, and reporting systems that help teams measure progress credibly.

What you get

A tailored KPI framework, indicator definitions, data collection guidance, dashboard recommendations, and reporting logic.

Best for

Teams that need to move beyond scattered metrics toward a usable measurement system.

Sustainability Reporting & Claims Review

Support to strengthen sustainability narratives, disclosures, and claims so they are credible, evidence-based, and aligned with what the organization can actually substantiate.

What you get

A claims risk review, evidence gap map, recommended revisions, and a clearer reporting structure.

Best for

Companies preparing external sustainability communications, reports, web copy, buyer responses, or investor-facing materials.

Supply Chain, Human Rights & Market Risk

For companies and suppliers operating in complex sourcing and market environments.

Supplier Sustainability Readiness

Support for suppliers and growing companies responding to buyer sustainability, ESG, responsible sourcing, or compliance expectations.

What you get

A readiness diagnostic, gap analysis, buyer questionnaire support, priority action list, and simple documentation structure.

Best for

Suppliers that want to look more credible to buyers without building an overcomplicated ESG function.

Human Rights Due Diligence & Responsible Sourcing

Advisory support to identify human rights, labor, and responsible sourcing risks across operations, partners, or supply chains.

What you get

A risk map, responsibility matrix, due diligence workflow, escalation points, and practical mitigation recommendations.

Best for

Companies exposed to labor, supplier, community, or operating-context risks.

New Market & Operating Context Risk

Support for organizations entering, expanding, or working in complex markets where political, social, regulatory, or stakeholder risks affect implementation.

What you get

A context risk brief, stakeholder map, operating assumptions, red flags, and decision recommendations.

Best for

Teams operating across emerging markets, fragile contexts, frontier markets, or cross-cultural stakeholder environments.

Responsible AI Adoption & Governance

For organizations that want to use AI well — and avoid the risks that come with using it poorly.

AI Readiness Diagnostic

A practical assessment of where AI could help your organization — and where it should not be used yet.

What you get

A readiness score, workflow opportunity map, risk flags, data sensitivity review, and prioritized AI use-case list.

Best for

Teams curious about AI but unsure where to begin safely.

AI Use-Case Prioritization

Support to identify which AI use cases are worth testing based on value, risk, feasibility, and staff capacity.

What you get

A ranked use-case matrix, pilot recommendations, workflow notes, and "do / don't use AI here" guidance.

Best for

Organizations that want to avoid random experimentation and focus on practical, low-risk wins.

Responsible AI Policy & Guardrails

Development of lightweight, practical guidance for responsible AI use across teams.

What you get

A draft AI use policy, staff guidance, risk categories, approval rules, data protection considerations, and escalation pathways.

Best for

Companies or organizations whose staff are already using AI informally and need clearer boundaries.

AI Workflow & Tool Adoption Support

Support to integrate AI tools into real workflows without overwhelming staff or creating avoidable risks.

What you get

Workflow redesign notes, tool recommendations, staff prompts, training materials, and implementation guidance.

Best for

Teams that want AI to save time without creating chaos, compliance risk, or low-quality outputs.
